## Captioned carefully, on purpose

`hot-work.jpg` shows **heat being applied to a bicycle frame**, which is what the
trading name means and what separates this workshop from a shop that only fits
parts. **It is captioned as hot work on a frame and nothing more specific.**
Brazing a lugged joint and welding a repair are different operations, and a
framebuilder would know at a glance which one a given photograph shows. Naming
the wrong one on a page whose entire argument is craft knowledge would be the
sort of error the audience notices first.

## The brand was removed by cropping, not by hoping

The servicing photograph originally had **SHIMANO** legible across the cassette.
Every candidate from that shoot did. **The frame was cropped to the hands, the
allen key and the derailleur**, which removes the lettering entirely rather than
relying on it being small. Component branding is incidental rather than a claim,
but the rule is the same either way: make it impossible, do not hope.

## Rejected

- **Welding rebar.** A beautiful arc-and-sparks photograph of concrete
  reinforcing bar. Nothing to do with bicycles, on a page about frames.
- **A wheel being trued in an alley**, barefoot in flip-flops against bare
  concrete. Visibly not a north London workshop, and it undercuts the expertise
  the page is arguing for.
