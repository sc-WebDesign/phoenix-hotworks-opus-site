The job other shops decline

When the frame itself is broken

A snapped stay or a cracked joint is usually where a bike's life ends, because fitting parts and repairing steel are different trades and most shops only do the first one. Here they are the same bench.

Whether a given frame is worth repairing depends on the frame, the damage and what it is made of. Bring it in or send a photograph and you will get a proper answer rather than a shrug.

Built to order

Frames, and teaching people to make them

Alongside the repairs, this is a framebuilding workshop. Steel frames built to fit the person who is going to ride them, including traditional lugged construction, which is the slow way and the reason the joints look like that.

There are also framebuilding courses run here, one to one, for people who want to make their own. Places, length and cost are all worth asking about directly rather than reading off a page.

If you have never commissioned a frame before, that is normal. It starts with a conversation about how you ride, not a specification.
